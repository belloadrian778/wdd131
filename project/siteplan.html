My Car Maintenance

WDD 131 - Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

My Car Maintenance

This name represents a website that helps car owners organize vehicle maintenance and expenses.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help car owners track maintenance services, record expenses, and learn about basic vehicle care.

Users can save their maintenance history and calculate how much money they spend on their cars.

Scenarios

  • How can I track my car maintenance expenses?
  • How can I save my vehicle service history?
  • What maintenance services should I perform regularly?

Color Schema

#17365D
#E5E7EB
#F59E0B

Dark blue will be used for headings, navigation, and the footer.

Light gray will be used for backgrounds and cards.

Orange will highlight buttons and important details.

Typography

Font: Arial, sans-serif

Arial will be used for headings, navigation, paragraphs, and buttons because it is easy to read.

Website Pages

  • Home: Website introduction and benefits.
  • Maintenance Tracker: Record services and expenses.
  • Maintenance Guide: Vehicle maintenance tips.
